How these numbers are calculated
  • Average daily sales — from the Maggies Tank_COE sheet, which Coefficient refreshes daily. Each SKU's month-to-date total is differenced between the 20 Aug and 25 Aug syncs (5.00 days) and the 13–20 Aug syncs (6.58 days), then blended 65/35 toward the more recent window. Dividing by the real gap between syncs rather than a flat 7 days keeps a part-day sync from understating velocity.
  • Total stock = current inventory + in-production inventory, as you asked. Note that no in-production quantity in the file carries a completion or warehouse arrival date, so this treats goods on a factory floor as equivalent to goods on a shelf.
  • Required stock = 14 days of demand (7-day supplier lead time + 7-day order cycle) + safety stock. Safety stock is SKU-specific: 1.96 × √(2 × σ_weekly² + daily² × 2²), driven by each SKU's own volatility, so a steady seller carries far less buffer than an erratic one.
  • Recommended order = required stock − total stock, never below zero. It is additional to everything you already hold and everything already in production.
  • Priority — Critical under 7 days of stock (runs out before a new order could land), High under 14 days, Medium under 21.
  • The 7-day lead time is an assumption you gave, not a measured figure. Goods described as "in production" imply a manufacturing lead time well beyond it; at 14 days every required-stock figure rises by roughly half.
  • SKUs with no recorded sales are listed but never forecast, and SKUs selling but absent from the inventory workbook cannot be checked here at all — mostly QL1047 variants, about 18 units a day.
  • SKUs beginning 134821 are excluded from every calculation.
