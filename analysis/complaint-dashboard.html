How these numbers were built

Processing order. Date range → CommSlayer product labels (return-exchange, size_issue, size_replacement, refund-request, damaged-item, fulfilment-error, product-suggestions) → conversations read → specific problem. Tickets that were only about shipping, tracking, order status, address changes, payment or fulfilment delays were excluded, as were pre-purchase questions and agent messages.

Counting. Each customer is counted once per specific problem. A customer reporting several distinct problems is counted under each. Percentages are customers reporting the problem ÷ all customers with a product complaint in the selected date range and store × 100, so the denominator stays fixed when you filter by category or problem and the shares remain comparable.

Scope and confidence. This build reads 64 conversations sampled across the labels above for 1–31 August 2026. Ranks and shares are reliable; absolute counts are sample counts, not the full month's total — August carried roughly 6,300 size-exchange and 1,900 fit-and-damage tickets in the same window. Problems are only named when the customer said where or how; anything vaguer is held as Unspecified rather than guessed.
